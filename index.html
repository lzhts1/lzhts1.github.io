Hello, I am
Zihan Liu

I am a Ph.D. student in the Class of 2027 at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). My research interests focus on generative world models, world behavior models, enterprise world models, and Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA). During my first undergraduate year, I was a member of my university's ACM competitive programming team. I have worked as a research assistant at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen; The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou); Wuhan University; and the Zhejiang University Binjiang Institute. I have also received RA offers from the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences; the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences; and Beihang University.
